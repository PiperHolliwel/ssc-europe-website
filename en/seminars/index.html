Seminars

Further education, summer schools and informational meetings for teachers, students and civil society actors.

The experts of SSC Europe have an extensive experience in conceptualising and holding seminars for different partners. We offer different formats for further education, summer schools and informational meetings.

Our experts have a broad portfolio of courses at their disposal. The seminars range from European integration over security policy to questions of public policy making. The members of the SSC Europe network have been teaching at renowned universities such as the Ludwig-Maximilians-University Munich, the Georg-August-University Göttingen, the Charles-University Prague, the Andrássy-University Budapest or the Chualongkorn-University Bangkok.

Our cooperations with partners include German political party foundations, private foundations and educational institutes. We furthermore work together closely with organisations from different branches worldwide. Some of them can be found here.
